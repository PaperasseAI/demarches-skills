# Visa long séjour étudiant

Coming to France to study for more than 90 days? The route is **Études en France
(Campus France) first where it applies, then France-Visas, then the visa centre**.
The visa issued is normally a **VLS-TS étudiant** — which must be validated online
within 3 months of arrival (see [validation-vls-ts](validation-vls-ts.md)).

## Éligibilité / Qui est concerné
- You must be **over 18** at the time of the stay.
- You must have **already chosen your course** — the official course/diploma
  catalogue is on campusfrance.org.
- You must be **accepted by a higher-education establishment**. How you enrol
  depends on your country of residence:
  - **Études en France (EEF) countries**: enrolment/acceptance runs through the
    **Études en France platform** (pastel.diplomatie.gouv.fr/etudesenfrance) —
    this is **mandatory before the visa application**; without the EEF procedure
    the consulate does not process the file.
  - Other countries: check enrolment conditions (specialities, level) on
    Campus France, then apply to the establishment directly.
- EEF countries as of 2026-07 (the list moves — confirm on the EEF platform or
  the France-Visas student page): most of Francophone and anglophone Africa
  (Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon…), North Africa/Middle East
  (Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia, Egypt, Iran, Israel, Gulf states…), much of Asia
  (China, India, Indonesia, Japan, Vietnam…), the Americas (USA, Brazil, Mexico,
  Canada…), and Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Russia, Turkey, Ukraine, the UK.

## Étapes
1. **(EEF countries) Complete Études en France** on the pastel platform:
   candidature, acceptance, and the Campus France interview/fees where required.
   Keep the EEF number — the consulate matches the visa file against it.
2. **Enter the France-Visas application** — always start from the
   **france-visas.gouv.fr home page**: the news banners there carry dated rule
   changes for students (thresholds, new upload flows). "Apply for a visa" from
   the header.
3. **Digitise the supporting documents at entry (recommended, 2026 flow)** —
   students can upload scans of the justificatifs while filling the online
   application; a complete, legible digital file earns a **faster dedicated
   counter** at the visa centre. See specs below.
4. **Book the appointment** at the in-country visa centre (TLScontact, VFS
   Global, Capago… — the operator per country is listed on
   france-visas.gouv.fr/lieu-de-depot), then **transmit the application** to the
   centre on France-Visas before the appointment day.
5. **Attend the appointment** (originals + copies, biometrics, fee) and follow
   the decision via "Follow an application".
6. **After arrival**: validate the VLS-TS within 3 months —
   [validation-vls-ts](validation-vls-ts.md).

## Pièces justificatives
- Passport, photo, acceptance/enrolment proof (EEF attestation or admission
  letter), accommodation proof, and **proof of resources**:
  - **Applications submitted on or after 2026-08-01: minimum 877,50 €/month**
    (France-Visas « Student campaign », July 2026; the previous base was
    ≈ 615 €/month). A file that is ready before that date falls under the
    previous, lower bar.
  - Typical evidence: ~3 months of bank statements (student's or a sponsor's,
    with the sponsor's ID and income proof) or a scholarship letter — the
    consulate's own checklist prevails.

### Numériser ses justificatifs (specs officielles, avr. 2026)
- Formats **pdf, png, jpeg/jpg** only; **in colour**; A4; the file must **not be
  password-protected** (re-export bank PDFs if needed).
- **≤ 5 Mo per file**, **≤ 15 Mo per pièce justificative**, **≤ 20 documents per
  rubrique** (remaining space is shown at each upload).
- **300 dpi** recommended, **600 dpi** for faint detail (passport stamps); every
  word legible, the **whole document in frame**, no shadows/flash/zoom blur —
  cropped or blurry scans are rejected.
- One file per document type, descriptive name ≤ 100 characters (alphanumerics
  incl. accents, space, apostrophe, hyphen, dot, comma); multi-page pieces
  suffixed `_p1`, `_p2` (ex : `passeport_p1.pdf`).

## Délais et échéances
- Apply at most **~3 months before travel**; allow several weeks of processing.
- **2026-08-01** is the pivot date for the resource floor (877,50 €/month for
  applications *submitted* from that date).
- After arrival: **3 months** to validate the VLS-TS.

## Portail / Où faire la démarche
- **france-visas.gouv.fr** — free and official; enter via the **home page** (the
  student news banner lands there). Beware paid look-alike "visa services".
- **pastel.diplomatie.gouv.fr/etudesenfrance** — the Études en France platform.
- campusfrance.org — course catalogue and per-country enrolment conditions
  (context).

## Pièges connus
- **Skipping Études en France** in an EEF country — the consulate will not
  process the visa file without it.
- **Partial digitisation**: the upload option is all-or-nothing (every requested
  justificatif). A missing or illegible scan is re-digitised by the operator at
  the appointment **and may be billed** as an ancillary service — check the
  operator's fee page when booking.
- **Password-protected bank PDFs** fail the upload requirements silently late.
- Budgeting on the old figure: from **2026-08-01** the resource floor is
  **877,50 €/month**, ~43 % higher than the previous base.
- Deep-linking into the site and missing the news banner that announces exactly
  this kind of change.
