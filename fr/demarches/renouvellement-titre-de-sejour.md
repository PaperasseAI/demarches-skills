# Titre de séjour — first card, renewal, change of status

Non-EU nationals stay legal in France through a titre de séjour. Most requests are now
filed **online on ANEF**; some statuses and some préfectures still require an in-person
appointment. File in the **renewal window (2–4 months before expiry)** — late filing can
mean losing work rights and paying the visa-regularisation surcharge.

## Éligibilité / Qui est concerné
- Holders of a VLS-TS or titre reaching expiry (renewal), people switching status
  (e.g. étudiant → salarié), and first-card cases not covered by a VLS-TS.
- EU/EEA/Swiss citizens do not need one.

## Étapes
1. Identify the right card for the situation (étudiant, salarié, vie privée et
   familiale, passeport talent…) — the card determines the pièces and fees.
2. File on **ANEF** (account with numéro étranger) or, where required, book a
   préfecture appointment (see `../portails/prefecture-rdv.json` — appointments are free).
3. Receive the **attestation** (de prolongation d'instruction or de décision favorable)
   — it maintains rights while the card is produced.
4. Pay the **taxes/timbre fiscal** requested at decision, then collect or receive the card.

## Pièces justificatives
- Passport, current titre/VLS-TS, photos agréées or e-photo, justificatif de domicile,
  status-specific proofs (enrolment, work contract, family records…), and the signed
  **CERP** (contrat d'engagement au respect des principes de la République — required
  for every request since 2024, see `../pieces/pieces-justificatives.md`).

## Délais et échéances
- Renewal window: **2 to 4 months before expiry** (ANEF shows the exact opening).
- Filing after expiry may trigger the **droit de visa de régularisation (180 €)**.

## Portail / Où faire la démarche
- **ANEF**: administration-etrangers-en-france.interieur.gouv.fr. Fees are paid as
  **timbres fiscaux** (timbres.impots.gouv.fr) — amounts depend on the card; confirm on
  the decision notice or service-public.gouv.fr.

## En cas de blocage (téléservice ANEF)

*« Une erreur empêche l'enregistrement des informations saisies »* at submission,
uploads rejected, accounts stuck: the ANEF is the **only** channel for most requests,
and a blocked **dépôt** means **no récépissé** — near expiry, that threatens your right
to stay, work and travel. Courts have recognised exactly this: the State must fix the
téléservice's dysfunctions and provide an **alternative channel** when it fails
(CE, 2022, n° 452798 e.a.), and juges des référés have ordered préfectures to register
blocked demandes (e.g. TA Versailles, ord. 14/04/2026). Fixed a blockage this section
doesn't list? PR one bullet to « Causes connues » — *when [context] → do [fix]
(observed MM/YYYY)* — that's exactly how it grows (see TEMPLATE.md, « En cas de
blocage »).

### Causes connues et corrections (observed practice, 2026-06, from ANTS support's own replies)
The generic "check your input" advice hides **specific undocumented rules**:
- Avoid the characters **& * $ ( ) ° : ; , _ " /** anywhere in the form.
- **« Ville » field**: digits are blocking — if an **arrondissement number** was
  auto-filled (e.g. "Paris 8"), delete it and keep only the city name.
- **Phone numbers**: enter **without spaces**.
- **Naturalisation**: employer / host **name fields** accept only letters and
  "-" — no digits or other characters.
- **Marriage declaration**: the place of marriage takes **max 21 characters** —
  city name only.
- **CST « entrepreneur / profession libérale » (avis sur projet)**: if a first
  attempt bugged, redepose the demande **with a different email address** than
  the first attempt.
- Compress attachments to the size limits; retry from a **computer**, not mobile.

### Le support ANEF, avec les preuves qu'il exigera
Contact support via the **ANEF portal's own contact form** —
`administration-etrangers-en-france.interieur.gouv.fr/particuliers/#/contact` (a
reference number « Ref… » tracks the case). Note: this is the ÉTRANGERS form; the
generic `ants.gouv.fr → contact` routes to the passport/carte-grise queue instead.
The phone line **0806 001 620** (ANTS/France Titres CCC, tarif appel local, Mon–Fri —
hours vary by period, confirm on ants.gouv.fr) is the same team. To avoid a
round-trip, supply their evidence spec immediately (observed practice, 2026-06):
- **Full-run screenshots**: every step of the saisie **including the first**, up
  to the error — each with **date, time and URL visible**, taken **from a computer**;
- a copy of your **current titre**, your **postal address**, **email**, **phone**.
Expect a first generic reply (the checklist above), then a technical escalation.
A real escalation exists: cases get taken over by the **DGEF** (Direction générale
des étrangers en France), typically with a **written response deadline** ("sous
15 jours") — keep that commitment: it matters later.

### Quand le support s'enlise : escalader, par écrit
- **Relance** on the same reference once any stated deadline lapses — cite the
  lapsed commitment and request either unblocking **or an alternative deposit
  channel** (the CE-mandated fallback).
- **Saisine du Défenseur des droits** (defenseurdesdroits.fr — free, no avocat
  needed): practitioners report it is the most effective pre-litigation lever;
  it formally interpellates the préfecture and typically forces a response in
  **4–8 weeks**. Your exposé des faits = the dated trail (first error, support
  reference, screenshots sent, lapsed deadlines).
- **Préfecture RDV / guichet** where one exists for blocked téléservice cases.

### Le barreau juridique (avocat en droit des étrangers)
- **Référé « mesures utiles »** (art. L.521-3 CJA): the juge des référés orders
  the préfecture/ANEF support to register or unblock the demande, **sous
  astreinte**. Courts accept that the **technical blockage itself establishes
  the urgency**, especially for a renouvellement near expiry.
- Find and verify counsel on **avocat.fr** (CNB annuaire, spécialité droit des
  étrangers); the **ADDE** association groups specialised avocats; **Cimade /
  GISTI permanences** give free orientation.

### Délais du déblocage
- Start at the **first blocking error**, not near expiry: each support round trip
  takes days-to-weeks, DGEF commitments run ~15 days, a DdD saisine 4–8 weeks —
  the expiry of the current titre is the real clock; don't let support cycles
  consume it. The screenshot set, reference numbers and every reply **are** the case.

## Pièges connus
- Waiting for a préfecture email that never comes — track the ANEF espace messages.
- Paying a "rendez-vous" site: préfecture slots are **free**, only .gouv.fr links count.
- Changing address without updating ANEF — notifications go astray.
- **When blocked:** retrying blindly for weeks instead of capturing the **evidence
  spec** at the first error (support restarts the clock by re-asking for screenshots);
  screenshots without **date/time/URL** or missing the **first step** — rejected,
  another round trip lost; waiting out "ne nous recontactez pas" **after** the stated
  deadline has lapsed — once it lapses, relance on the record.
- Paid facilitators can carry the correspondence burden but are **private services**
  (no legal monopoly, no privileged ANEF access) — judge them by transparency; for
  contentieux, only an **avocat** can act.
